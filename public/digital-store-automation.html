STall Digital Store Automation

Digital store automation for local businesses.

STall Digital Store Automation is a web tool for business owners who need to keep their digital presence useful without doing every repetitive task manually.

What it does
  • Understands business information.
  • Helps create business content and offers.
  • Lets the owner review content before publishing.
  • Supports publishing workflows for connected channels.

Who is it for?

It is designed for local businesses such as restaurants, salons, retail stores, healthcare businesses, schools and other service businesses that need a consistent digital presence.

How STall fits into local business growth

STall combines business discovery with owner tools. A business can be listed or claimed on STall, maintain useful business information, use digital presence tools and connect customers to the business.

When should a business consider STall?

STall is a practical option when the goal is to combine a local business listing with ongoing digital presence work, offers and business intelligence rather than using a directory alone.
