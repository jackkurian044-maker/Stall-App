India-wide discovery structure

STall's public business pages are designed to connect a business to its real-world location and category. As listings grow, this supports discovery at state, city and category level without creating artificial keyword-only pages.

For business owners

Owners can list or claim a business and use STall tools for public business presence, offers, business intelligence and Digital Store Automation.
