Guide: make a local business page genuinely useful

Start with the questions a customer is likely to ask before choosing a business. Confirm the real business name, correct category, location or service area, current hours, contact details and the services actually offered. If any detail is uncertain, verify it with the owner before publishing.

  1. Check the facts. Correct inconsistent names, phone numbers, hours, service details and location information.
  2. Explain the offer. Describe the service in plain language and add limitations or conditions the customer should know.
  3. Use genuine assets. Add authentic photos with suitable permissions and descriptive alternative text where supported.
  4. Check contact routes. Open phone, booking, map and website links on a mobile device.
  5. Review periodically. Recheck information after changes to hours, services, location or promotions.

Completeness does not guarantee a higher search position, but it reduces avoidable confusion and makes business information easier for customers to interpret.

Guide: use digital visibility data without misreading it

Search impressions indicate that a page appeared for a query; clicks show that someone selected it. A page can receive impressions without clicks, and average position can change as the mix of queries, locations and devices changes.

  • Separate brand searches from generic category searches.
  • Review impressions, clicks, click-through rate and average position together.
  • Compare similar periods and note major site changes.
  • Connect search activity to qualified enquiries, calls, bookings or completed sign-ups.
  • Do not treat a few days of data as proof that an SEO change caused a result.

Guide: compare nearby businesses fairly

Choose the correct category and a sensible local context before comparing results. Do not compare men's salons against unisex salons, or mix hospitals with unrelated healthcare listings. Keep preschools separate from schools when their services and customers differ. Missing data should be recorded as unknown, not treated as a negative result.

Use comparison to identify questions worth checking: is the service description clear, are hours available, is the offer current, or is the contact path useful? Confirm each improvement is accurate and within the owner's control.
