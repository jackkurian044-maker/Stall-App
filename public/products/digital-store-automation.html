Common questions

Does STall publish automatically to every platform?

No. Publishing depends on the channel, account connection and permissions enabled for the business. Check the available actions in your account before relying on a workflow.

Can it guarantee higher rankings or more customers?

No. Accurate, useful updates can support business communication, but rankings, AI recommendations, enquiries and sales depend on many factors and are not guaranteed.

What should I verify before publishing an offer?

Confirm the product or service, price, dates, eligibility, exclusions, location and contact or booking link. Do not publish an offer the business cannot honour.

Keep publishing under owner control

Digital Store Automation is designed to reduce repetitive work without removing the owner's review. Create a post or offer, check that the wording and details are correct, approve it, and publish through a supported destination.

Available publishing actions depend on the channels and connections enabled for the account. Review the resulting status in STall and correct any business details before publishing again.

A safe, repeatable content workflow

Automation is most useful when it saves repetitive effort without publishing inaccurate business information. A practical workflow is:

  1. Start from verified services, hours, contact details and offer conditions.
  2. Prepare a draft for a specific purpose, audience and channel.
  3. Check names, prices, dates, claims, links and any image permissions.
  4. Have the owner or authorised person approve the content.
  5. Publish through a supported connection and confirm the resulting status.

Keep a simple record of which message was published and whether it led to useful actions, such as website visits, calls or genuine enquiries. Avoid duplicating the exact same message everywhere when channel audiences and requirements differ.

Available publishing destinations depend on the account's current connections and permissions. Review the product status rather than assuming every social or search platform is connected.
