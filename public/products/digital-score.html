How to use your Digital Score

  1. Review the information and signals included in your report.
  2. Separate missing or inconsistent details from improvement opportunities.
  3. Choose practical actions your business can complete and verify.
  4. Review the assessment again as your digital presence changes.

The score is a guide to available information and improvement opportunities. It is not a guarantee of Google rankings, customer enquiries or sales.

What should a business do with the result?

Use a Digital Score as an assessment of the information and digital signals available to the report, not as a promise of rankings or sales. The exact evidence depends on the assessment and connections available at the time.

  • Separate incomplete business information from broader improvement opportunities.
  • Prioritise corrections that affect customer confidence, such as incorrect hours or contact details.
  • Check that important public pages explain the service, location and next step clearly.
  • Turn a finding into one owner-assigned action with a clear completion check.
  • Reassess after meaningful changes and compare like-for-like periods where possible.

For ongoing work, combine the findings with Business Discovery to maintain the public listing, Business Intelligence to review relevant nearby peers, or Digital Store Automation to help manage recurring content and offer workflows.
