What information makes a business listing useful?

A useful listing helps a customer answer a few simple questions: Is this the right business? What does it provide? Where is it located or what area does it serve? When can I contact it? What should I do next?

  • Use the real business name and the closest accurate category.
  • Keep the location or service area, phone number and opening hours up to date.
  • Describe the services or products in plain language rather than repeating search terms.
  • Show current offers only when their conditions and dates are clear.
  • Use genuine photos and check that links and contact routes work.

After claiming a listing

Review the information periodically, especially after changes to hours, services, contact details or offers. A claim or a public page is only useful when the owner keeps the details accurate. Visibility varies by the query, location, competition and information available, so a listing should not be treated as a ranking guarantee.

Eligibility, ownership and quality checks

Business Discovery is intended for genuine local businesses and accurate public information. Before claiming or sharing a listing, confirm that you are authorised to represent the business and that the details match the real-world business. Do not create duplicate listings to target extra search terms or misrepresent a service area.

If the business changes its name, phone number, opening hours, services or address, update the public information and check that contact links still work. Use the correct category and explain the services actually offered. A clear listing helps visitors decide whether to contact or visit the business, but visibility also depends on factors outside the listing.

Keep a simple maintenance routine: check critical details monthly and after any business change, remove expired offers, review user-facing links on mobile and ask the owner to verify details before publishing sensitive changes.
