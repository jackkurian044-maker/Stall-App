How STall supports local businesses

STall combines customer-facing local business discovery with owner-side tools for managing and improving digital presence. Business Discovery helps people find businesses and gives owners a way to create or claim an eligible public listing. Digital Score reviews the digital-presence signals available to the assessment and highlights potential improvements. Business Intelligence supports comparisons with relevant nearby businesses in the same category. Digital Store Automation helps owners prepare and manage supported content and offer workflows.

The products address related but distinct tasks. A business should first make sure its public information is accurate, then use available assessment or comparison data to choose an improvement, and finally maintain useful content where appropriate. Comparisons should not mix unlike categories; for example, men's salons should be evaluated separately from unisex salons, and preschools separately from schools.

STall is designed to complement existing channels such as search, maps, marketplaces and social networks rather than claim to replace them. Available features and signals depend on the account's connections and the information available. STall does not promise specific rankings, enquiries or sales.
