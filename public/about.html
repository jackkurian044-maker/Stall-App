STall is an India-wide platform connecting people with local businesses while giving business owners practical tools for visibility, digital intelligence and automation.

What STall is

STall brings local discovery, business listings, digital performance insights, competitive intelligence and digital store automation into one connected journey.

Who STall serves

Restaurants, unisex salons, men’s salons, retail stores, healthcare businesses, hospitals, schools, preschools, gyms and local services.

Our approach

  • Make local businesses easier to discover.
  • Turn digital signals into understandable scores and insights.
  • Compare businesses with relevant same-category peers.
  • Help owners act through practical AI-powered tools.
