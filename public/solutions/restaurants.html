Common questions

Is STall only for restaurants?
No. STall is designed for many local-business categories; the restaurant workflow is one focused use case.

Does STall replace Google, Zomato or Swiggy?
No. STall complements existing discovery and ordering channels by focusing on the business owner's digital presence, visibility gaps and competitive context.

Can owners claim a restaurant listing?
Yes. STall supports business listing and claiming so owners can manage their presence.

What should a restaurant update first for digital discoverability?
Start with accurate name, category, address, hours, menu or service details, contact or ordering links and current offer terms. Correct basic facts before increasing posting frequency.

Does STall guarantee more diners or higher Google rankings?
No. STall helps organise digital presence and review improvement opportunities, but rankings, visits and sales are not guaranteed.

Can a restaurant compare itself with any nearby business?
The comparison should use relevant nearby restaurants in a matching category. Unrelated categories and unavailable information should not be treated as like-for-like evidence.
