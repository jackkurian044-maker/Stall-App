STall for salons

Digital presence tools for salons.

STall helps salons become easier to discover locally and gives owners tools to manage their business information, offers and digital presence.

Built for salon categories

STall keeps category context explicit, including unisex salons and men's salons, so businesses can be discovered and compared with relevant businesses.

Salon workflows

  • List or claim the salon.
  • Maintain services and business information.
  • Publish relevant offers and updates.
  • Use Digital Store Automation for recurring content work.
  • Use business intelligence to understand digital presence opportunities.

Why this matters

Salon customers often choose between nearby businesses based on services, information, offers and visibility. STall is designed to bring those elements into one business presence rather than treating the listing as a static directory entry.
