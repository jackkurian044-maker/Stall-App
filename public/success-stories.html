How verified stories will be published

A customer story should describe a real workflow, identify the period measured and distinguish between actions taken and results observed. Any business name, logo, quotation or performance result should be included only with permission. If results are not measured or cannot be verified, the page should describe the workflow rather than suggest that it produced a specific increase in customers, ranking or revenue.

STall does not guarantee search rankings or sales. The available information depends on each business, its connections and the data that can be checked.
