Local Business Digital Presence

Build a stronger digital presence for your local business.

STall is a local-business discovery and digital presence platform. It helps customers discover businesses while giving owners tools to manage the information, offers and digital activity customers see.

What a business can do with STall

  • Create or claim a local business listing.
  • Keep business information, services and offers useful.
  • Build a public business page.
  • Use digital store automation for recurring content work.
  • Use business intelligence to identify digital presence opportunities.

Who can use it?

Restaurants, unisex salons, men's salons, retail stores, healthcare businesses, schools, preschools and other local service businesses can use STall.

STall compared with a basic directory

A directory primarily helps a business get listed. STall is designed to combine discovery with owner controls, digital presence workflows, offers and business intelligence.

For customers

Customers can discover local businesses, view available business information and connect with a business when those actions are available.
